## Overview

This Introduction to Mechatronics course project combined a mobile collection prototype with OpenCV and an AI model trained by the team to detect trash. The chassis reused a modular design developed approximately two years earlier for the seminar Autonomous Guided Vehicle project.

The supplied media documents one bottle-detection output, the chassis during assembly, and two physical demonstrations. The training dataset, model architecture, collection sequence, team size, individual ownership boundaries beyond mechanical and electronics development, and validated results remain to be documented.
