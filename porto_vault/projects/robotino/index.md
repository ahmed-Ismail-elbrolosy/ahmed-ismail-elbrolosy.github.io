## Overview

Robotino is a three-wheel omnidirectional mobile robot developed from kinematic derivation through mechanical fabrication and embedded integration.

## Kinematics and mechanical design

Closed-form kinematic equations were derived and validated for omnidirectional motion. The system was designed in SolidWorks, then built with a 3D-printed load-bearing chassis and silicon-molded mecanum wheels.

## Embedded and perception stack

An ESP32 running micro-ROS connects the mobile platform to the software stack. OpenCV plant detection and a seven-parameter soil sensor support autonomous field-condition assessment.

## Simulation and integration

A digital twin connected the kinematic model, mechanical assembly, embedded behavior, and physical prototype.
